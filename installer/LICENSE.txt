READER SIMULATOR - LICENCE AND DISCLAIMER

PLEASE READ
Reader Simulator is an unofficial tool. It is not made by, affiliated with or
endorsed by Xteink. The simulator is approximate: screens, speed, battery and
buttons can differ from a real reader. Use it at your own risk.

Reader Simulator downloads ready-made simulator builds of e-reader firmwares
from a list on GitHub, over HTTPS, and checks every download with SHA-256.
It has no telemetry and no automatic updates. Each firmware belongs to its
authors and comes with its own licence (LICENSE-firmware.txt in its folder).
The simulator library is crosspoint-simulator / crossink-simulator by Julia
Nguyen (uxjulia) and the crosspoint-reader community (MIT licence).
