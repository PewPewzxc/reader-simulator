Controls

Use the drawn reader like the real one: press its keys, and on the X4 Pro touch its screen.

Keys on the reader

  • ClickPress the key
  • HoldLong press, e.g. Power to sleep
  • Click while asleepWake up

Try it: every new SD card has two free books and an English dictionary. On the X4 Pro with VARAGH, hold a word in a book to look it up, or turn on Settings › Reader › Tap to Look Up to look words up with a tap. In CrossInk, use Look Up Word in the reader menu. Two-finger gestures cannot be made with one pointer.

About

Reader Simulator

E-reader firmwares for the Xteink X4 Pro, X4 Classic and X3, running in your browser. Each one is compiled from its public source code with a desktop simulator library, then to WebAssembly.

    Privacy

    Nothing leaves your browser. Books you add and the simulated SD cards stay in this browser's storage; no account, no tracking. The Buy me a coffee button's picture comes from Buy Me a Coffee's server.

    Disclaimer

    Unofficial. Not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Xteink. The simulator is an approximation: speed, screen refresh, battery, Wi-Fi and some hardware differ from a real reader. Official Xteink firmware cannot run here. Provided as is, without warranty.
