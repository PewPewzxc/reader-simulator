How to use the reader

Drawn keys

Click a key
Press it (Back, Confirm, Left, Right, Up, Down, Power, Home on the X4 Pro)
Hold a key
Long press: keep the mouse button down. Power: sleep. Back in a book: home or another action set in the firmware. Confirm: quick menu, where the firmware has one
Click a key while asleep
Wake the reader

Touchscreen (X4 Pro)

Tap
Click the screen: buttons, list items, links, tap-to-look-up a word
Hold
Keep the mouse button down on the screen for half a second: in a book this selects the word (look it up, or select a sentence, as the firmware allows; the sample SD card has a dictionary)
Swipe left / right
Drag quickly (under 0.7 s): next / previous page
Swipe up
Drag quickly upwards: reader menu (X4 Pro firmwares that have one)
Swipe down from the top
Start at the top edge of the screen: light panel, or close a menu
Slow drag
Move sliders, scroll lists

Two-finger gestures cannot be made with a mouse.

About

Reader Simulator runs e-reader firmwares made for the Xteink X4 Pro, X4 Classic and X3 in your browser. Each firmware is compiled from its public source code together with a desktop simulator library, then to WebAssembly. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your browser: books you add and the simulated SD card stay in this browser's storage.
